# Direction artistique — Startup Act KPI Dashboard

## Three Directions

### Theme Name: Civic Ledger
Very brief intro: Un tableau de bord de politique publique inspiré des registres administratifs modernisés : papier ivoire, bleu d’encre, accents safran et hiérarchie éditoriale. L’ensemble doit évoquer la preuve, la traçabilité et la lecture rapide.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Signal Room
Very brief intro: Une salle de contrôle analytique à fond graphite, avec des signaux colorés et des cartes métriques très lisibles. La direction favorise la densité maîtrisée sans tomber dans le cyberpunk décoratif.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Mediterranean Index
Very brief intro: Un langage plus lumineux et territorial, inspiré des archives méditerranéennes, des bleus de faïence et du sable chaud. Il rend les données institutionnelles plus humaines et accessibles.
Probability: 0.05

## Selected Approach: Civic Ledger

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial information design, influenced by Swiss editorial grids and public-sector data books.

### Core Principles
1. Every number needs provenance: show source, period, and quality status.
2. Dense information is organized through rhythm, not decoration.
3. Warm paper surfaces support trust; ink-blue structure supports authority.
4. Exceptions and missing values are visible, not hidden.

### Color Philosophy
The ownable color is **archive saffron** (#E9A23B), used only for highlights, warnings, and selected states. Deep ink (#18263D) anchors the interface, while warm paper (#F6F1E8) prevents the dashboard from feeling like a generic SaaS console. Moss green indicates validated decisions; terracotta flags quality gaps.

### Layout Paradigm
A persistent narrow left rail anchors navigation and provenance. The main canvas uses an asymmetric editorial layout: a wide KPI ledger, a narrow “signal” column, and charts arranged like annotated report plates rather than equal cards.

### Signature Elements
1. A saffron “ledger rule” under key totals.
2. Small source stamps showing `85 sessions`, `JSON`, `Excel`, and `vérifié`.
3. Dense, numbered KPI tiles with a thin vertical index marker.

### Interaction Philosophy
Filters should feel like changing a report scope, not launching a modal. Session and year selectors update the whole canvas. Hover states reveal source context and definitions. Quality warnings remain visible and actionable.

### Animation
Use restrained 180–240ms transitions. KPI totals fade and rise subtly on scope changes. Chart series draw in with a short opacity/translate reveal. Respect reduced motion and never animate layout dimensions.

### Typography System
Use **DM Sans** for UI and data labels, paired with **Source Serif 4** for the main title and editorial section headings. KPI values use DM Sans with tabular numerals. Headline hierarchy: 48px title, 24px section heads, 13px uppercase metadata, 12px source stamps.

### Brand Essence
The trusted operating picture of Startup Act decisions, for program teams and analysts who need to see 85 sessions without losing the evidence behind each number. Personality: **precise, civic, candid**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are declarative and evidence-led. CTAs are practical, never promotional. Microcopy explains where a number comes from.
Example lines: “85 sessions, one traceable view.” / “Compare the decision signal before you open the detail.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact monogram built from three stacked ledger lines, with the middle line offset into a saffron notch. It should work as a small mark in the sidebar without relying on text.

### Signature Brand Color
Archive saffron — `#E9A23B`.
